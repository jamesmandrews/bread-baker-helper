Dough is not measured by how much fills the pan solid — it goes in at roughly half and rises the rest of the way. What matters is the share of the pan's capacity you start with: length × width × height × fill.

30 × 10 × 10 cm = 3000 cm³, at 45% = 1350 g.

40–45%
Standard sandwich loaf
50–55%
Enriched or deliberately fuller loaves

A pan's capacity in cm³ is the same number as the grams of water it holds to the brim, so you can measure a pan on a scale instead of with a ruler.

Sources vary at the top of the range. Divide a dough weight you already know works by that pan's volume to get your own figure — three of your own pans beats any published table.
