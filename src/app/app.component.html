Flour is always 100%. Every other ingredient is a percentage of the total flour weight, so a recipe scales to any batch size without changing how it behaves.

70% hydration means 700 g water per 1000 g flour.

} @if (activeTab() === 'pan-volume') {

Dough for a loaf pan is measured against the volume it has to fill: length × width × height × density.

30 × 10 × 10 cm = 3000 cm³, at 0.60 g/cm³ = 1800 g.

These density presets are unverified and look high — loaf-pan practice is nearer 0.35–0.45 g/cm³. Check the result against a pan you know before trusting it.
