How it works:

  • Enter your pan dimensions: Length × Width × Height (in centimeters)
  • Select a dough density preset or enter a custom value
  • The calculator determines dough weight: Volume × Density
  • Typical dough densities: Light (0.55 g/cm³), Standard (0.60 g/cm³), Dense (0.65 g/cm³)
  • Example: 30cm × 10cm × 10cm = 3000 cm³ × 0.60 g/cm³ = 1800g of dough
