How it works:

  • Select a pizza style preset or enter a custom dough loading factor
  • Enter your pan diameter(s) in inches
  • The calculator determines the dough weight based on pan area × dough loading
  • Dough loading values: Thin (2.55 g/in²), Medium (3.40 g/in²), Thick (5.10 g/in²), Detroit (6.24 g/in²)
  • Based on traditional factors: 0.09-0.22 oz/in² converted to grams
